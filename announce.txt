Updated for the new FlightKeys format
CargoDecode now reads the new FlightKeys OFP. Open the app once with a connection and it updates itself — the Flight Log will tell you which format it read.

Found a problem? Use the "Report an issue" button at the bottom of the app, or email CargoAeroData@gmail.com. If a number looks wrong, trust the paper and tell us.

Hope you enjoy the app. Please keep sharing it with the people you fly with — and if there is something you want it to do, email us. Most of what is in here started as somebody saying "it should just…".
